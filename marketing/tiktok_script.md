# TikTok / Instagram Reel Script

## Duration: 30-45 seconds
## Format: POV / screen recording + face cam

---

## Hook (0-3s) — make them stop scrolling:
**[Hold up a mystery Nespresso pod to camera]**
"Nobody actually knows what this pod is"

---

## Build (3-15s):
**[Open BrewScan, point camera at pod]**
"I built an app for this"
**[Scan animation plays, ring locks onto pod]**
"Watch"
**[Tap scan button]**

---

## Payoff (15-30s):
**[Results screen appears: pod name, tasting notes, intensity]**
"Ristretto. Intensity 10 out of 13. Cereal and biscuity notes. Pairs well with milk."
**[Scroll through brew tips and recipes]**
"It even tells you what to make with it"

---

## CTA (30-45s):
"It's called BrewScan. Free on the App Store."
**[Text overlay: BrewScan — link in bio]**

---

## Caption:
POV: you finally know what's in your Nespresso drawer ☕🔍
#nespresso #coffee #ai #coffeeapp #nespressorecipes #brewtok #coffeelover

## Hashtag strategy:
Primary: #nespresso #nespressocommunity #nespressorecipes
Secondary: #coffeeapp #ai #brewtok #coffeelover #espresso
Trending (check day-of): #coffeetime #coffeetiktok

---

## Filming tips:
- Film in good lighting (kitchen counter works great)
- Use the actual scan animation — it looks satisfying on camera
- The results card is the money shot — hold on it for 3-4 seconds
- Reaction face when it identifies correctly = gold
