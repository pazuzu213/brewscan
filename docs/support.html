☕ Support

Need help? We're here for you.

Camera not working?

Go to Settings → BrewScan → Camera and make sure camera access is enabled.

Pod not identified?

Try these tips for a better scan:

  • Make sure you have good lighting
  • Hold the pod steady in the center of the viewfinder
  • Remove the pod from its packaging if possible
  • Try scanning the top or side label

Subscription issues?

Tap Profile → Restore Purchases to restore a previous subscription. You can also manage your subscription via Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

App crashing or not loading?

Force-quit the app and reopen it. If the issue persists, delete and reinstall from the App Store — your saved scans are backed up to your account.
