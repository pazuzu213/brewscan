Common Questions

Camera not working?

Go to Settings → BrewScan → Camera and enable access.

Pod not identified?

Ensure good lighting and hold the pod steady in the centre of the viewfinder. Try removing the pod from packaging.

Subscription issues?

Tap Profile → Restore Purchases, or manage via Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
