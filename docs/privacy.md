# Privacy Policy — BrewScan

**Last updated: August 16, 2026**

## Overview

BrewScan ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data We Collect

### Camera
BrewScan requests camera access solely to photograph Nespresso capsules for AI identification. Photos are sent to OpenAI's API for analysis and are not stored on our servers.

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. This is stored locally on your device.

### Scan History
Your scan history, saved recipes, and favourite pods are stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to any server.

### Purchase Information
Subscription purchases are processed by Apple. We do not collect or store payment information.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect precise location data
- We do not track you across other apps or websites
- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not use your data for advertising

## Third-Party Services

**OpenAI** — Pod images are sent to OpenAI's API for analysis. OpenAI's privacy policy applies: [openai.com/privacy](https://openai.com/privacy)

**Apple StoreKit** — Subscription management is handled by Apple.

## Data Retention

All personal data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.
