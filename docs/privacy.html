☕ PodScan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Camera

PodScan uses your camera solely to photograph Nespresso capsules for AI identification. Images are sent to OpenAI's API and are not stored on our servers.

Account Information

Your name and email are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload personal information to any server.

Scan History & Favourites

All scan history, saved recipes, and favourite pods are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store payment information.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not track you across apps or websites
  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not collect location data

Third-Party Services

OpenAI — Pod images are processed by OpenAI. See openai.com/privacy.
